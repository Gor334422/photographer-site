Why a first look makes your wedding day calmer

12.09.2026

A first look is a private moment before the ceremony when you see each other alone. Without guests watching, the nerves settle and the real reactions happen.

It also frees up time. Portraits are done before the party, so you can enjoy cocktails and dinner with your guests.

Planning your portraits around golden hour

28.08.2026

Golden hour is the hour before sunset, when the light turns soft and warm. In Armenia it falls around 7 pm in summer and 4:30 pm in winter.

Ask your venue when the sun sets and keep 30 minutes free for portraits just before it. Even a short walk in that light gives you your best photographs.

A simple timeline for an 8-hour wedding

10.07.2026

Start with 90 minutes for getting ready and details, then 30 minutes for a first look. Allow 45 minutes for the ceremony and 60 minutes for family and couple portraits.

Keep the last three hours for dinner, speeches and the first dance. Leave a 15-minute buffer between each part, because weddings rarely run exactly on time.
